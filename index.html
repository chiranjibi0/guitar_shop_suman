Services

Our Services

Full Guitar Service

A complete check-up and maintenance service for your guitar. This includes cleaning, string adjustment, neck alignment, action setup, intonation correction, and overall performance optimization to make your guitar play and sound its best.

Bridge Fixing

We repair or replace damaged or loose bridges to restore tuning stability, proper string height, and perfect tone transfer.

Truss Rod Adjustment

Precise neck adjustment to fix bending, buzzing, or high action, ensuring perfect neck balance and playability.

Pickup Replacement

Upgrade or replace guitar pickups to improve sound quality, volume balance, and tone character according to your playing style.

Re-Fretting

Complete replacement of worn-out frets to restore smooth playing, accurate notes, and professional performance.

Re-Crowning

Frets are reshaped and polished after leveling to improve intonation, sustain, and playing comfort.

Re-Wiring

Repair or replace internal wiring to fix noise, signal loss, grounding problems, and ensure clean sound output.

Electric Pot Cleaning

Cleaning and servicing volume and tone pots to remove crackling noise and ensure smooth control operation.
